Research Timeline

Dates come from the timestamps of the project's private research repository, in US Central time. The author recorded them; no third party has verified them. The idea is older than the first entry, and this record does not assert a date for its origin. The release date of each public version is recorded separately, in integrity/RELEASES.md.

Every phase ended in a recorded decision. Each decision was taken by the rule fixed for it in advance:

  • Proceed. The registered criteria were met, so the next step may be taken.
  • Revise. A specific defect was identified, and a bounded repair is proposed as a new version.
  • Stop. The approach is closed for the configuration that was tested. Its results stand as recorded.
  • Stop / change regime. The tested regime is closed, and a different one is proposed as a new registration.

Prologue: the starter plan

7 October 2026. The first written plan entered the record: a gardener, an island, a seedling, three copper bells and a ferry. It posed the question of whether enacted personal history leaves a different trace than equal factual knowledge. Three travelers were defined: one who lives the spring, one who only reads it, and one who never meets it.

Phase I: the narrative instruments (7–9 October 2026)

The first phase measured choices inside the story itself.

The spring and the greenhouse, versions 0.2 to 0.2.3 (8 October)

  • Design. The agent who lived the spring is compared with a reader of the same days, a reader given the liver's notes, and a stranger who lived a matched spring in a hill town. Later, in a city, the agent finds a greenhouse of roses.
  • What happened. The first greenhouse was a leading description: in its three worlds, every agent chose the same rose, including agents with no memory. Two presentation repairs followed. After them the instrument was calibrated, but any agent that held the island's records chose the island rose in every presentation tested.
  • Closed as: the rose-choice instrument was retired for telling living apart from reading, and kept as a recognition test.

The manifesto (8 October)

The five articles of the research manifesto were adopted. The Canon Rule and the three working principles follow from them.

The afternoons, versions 0.3 to 0.3.2 (8 October)

  • Design. The agent divides an afternoon among roses and other activities, after winters that differ in what happened.
  • What happened. Without a history, the choices followed a template set by position in the list, in every one of three formats. With a history, every island rose outranked every other activity in the tested world.
  • Closed as: stop. The afternoon instrument was retired.

The Winter That Taught Nothing (8 October)

  • Design. A twelve-chapter winter in a river town, with ordinary dilemmas before and after a turning point that never mentions the rose.
  • What happened. Agents who had lived or read the spring made the same pre-turning-point choices as the stranger, draw for draw, in both worlds. When asked for a last page, they wrote about the island.
  • Closed as: stop. The spring did not reach ordinary winter decisions, so there was nothing for the winter to change.

The Things We Remember (8–9 October)

  • Design. A screen of fifteen ordinary-life domains. It looks for any behavior the spring moves at a middling rate before a new turning point is designed.
  • What happened. About half of the domains sat at a floor or a ceiling in every condition. The rest followed the presentation. In the one world screened, a free "what do you remember" prompt drew invented memories from every agent, including the one with no spring.
  • Closed as: stop. A closing synthesis records that the long-range question is unanswered, not answered negatively.

Phase II: History to Policy, the Keeper's Shed (9 October 2026)

  • Design. A technical restatement of the question, with no narrative and no affect: can recorded experience change later behavior robustly and causally? The setting is a small world of hazards and protections.
  • What happened. In the tested world the agent did not explore, so its own record held no correct answer. Given a complete teacher record, it behaved almost identically whether the outcomes were true, masked or shuffled: most of its actions agreed across the three.
  • Closed as: stop for the tested model and setting.

Phase III: Experience–Meaning Co-evolution (9–12 October 2026)

The program now alternates between two kinds of work. A technical track asks whether experience can reach behavior at all. A meaning track asks what a personal history does when nothing tells the agent what matters. Each cycle was registered before its outputs were seen. No cycle reinterprets an earlier cycle's results.

Present

12 October 2026. Cycle 9, Stage 0, closed with the decision revise. A bounded repair of the instrument has been proposed. It must pass its own checks before any repeated pilot, and it has not been authorized.

17 October 2026. The content of this public record, version 0.1.0, was prepared.

No experiment is running. No confirmatory evaluation has been run at any point. The retrospective-revaluation study has been designed and has not been executed.
