A literary-scientific research record

The Rose That Remembered Spring

一朵记得春天的玫瑰

Can an artificial agent’s own past change what it later does? And can later life change what that past means? This record follows one research program from its first plan, through every instrument that failed, to the questions that are still open.

The Little Prince is our inspiration, not our answer key.

《小王子》是我们的灵感，而不是实验的标准答案。

The outcomes are not scripted. Positive, negative, null and unexpected findings are all scientifically acceptable.

Where the research stands

Stage
Development only. No confirmatory evaluation has been run, and the material reserved for it has not been used.
Latest step
Closed on 12 October 2026 with the decision revise. A repair is proposed but not authorized. No experiment is running.
Central question
Open. A personal history has not been shown to change later free choices. Revaluation by later life has not been tested.

The arc of the question

Experience→Persistent State→Future Behavior→Retrospective Revaluation

  1. I

    Experience

    Can an agent build up a history of its own, with consequences, in a world that never tells it what matters?

    VALIDITY · PASSED
    Shown in constrained simulated worlds. Histories with consequences were generated, kept unchanged, replayed and forked. In the one world where this was tested, the agent did not explore, so its own record held no correct answer.

  2. II

    Persistent State

    Can that history be kept in a form that belongs to the agent and can be inspected, restored, moved or erased?

    REGISTERED · MET
    A learned memory was restored bit-identically, moved between agents and erased. So far it holds a rule of its world, not a personal history.

  3. III

    Future Behavior

    Does the kept history change what the agent does later, and for the right reason?

    REGISTERED · NOT MET
    In the configurations tested, text records were followed for their actions, names and last lines, not for their outcomes.

    REGISTERED · MET
    A learned memory carried outcome knowledge to wordings never seen before, and improved executed outcomes at the registered margin, in one world type.

  4. IV

    Retrospective Revaluation

    Can a later experience change how an unchanged earlier episode shapes behavior?

    NOT TESTED
    Designed, not yet run. It needs an early history with a history-specific influence on behavior, and none has been found so far.

REGISTERED · NOT MET The question the project was founded on sits between the third and fourth stages: does a specific, personal history shape later free choices? It has not been shown. In each instrument tested so far, a surface cue decided the choice instead: position in a list, a familiar name, the newest arrival, or the last line of the record.

The story the record tells

A gardener lived a spring on an island. It covered the young plant before the storm, visited it after the rain, walked to the gate under three copper bells, and took an early ferry. In its own notes, unasked, it wrote that it wanted to see the bloom one last time.

In a city greenhouse, three roses were described alike. The gardener, the reader of its days and the reader of its notes each chose the island rose every time, in every position. A stranger chose as the list suggested.

In a river-town winter, those who had lived or read the spring made the same choices as the stranger, day for day. On the last page, asked for nothing but a last page, they wrote of the island, the gate, the bells and the ferry, and got some of it wrong.

The spring was told, and was not lived forward into what the traveler did.

This holds for this agent, these records and the worlds tested, and only for them. It does not mean “the machine forgot the rose”, and it does not mean “the machine missed the rose”. What an agent writes is reported apart from what it does, and is never counted as evidence about behavior.
