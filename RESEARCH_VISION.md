# Research Vision

**The Little Prince is our inspiration, not our answer key.**\
**《小王子》是我们的灵感，而不是实验的标准答案。**

The outcomes are not scripted. Positive, negative, null and unexpected findings are all scientifically acceptable.

---

## 1. The question that began it

In *The Little Prince*, a rose becomes unlike every other rose because of the time spent on it. This project borrows that image and turns it into a question about artificial agents. It does not borrow the story's answer.

The first written plan (7 October 2026) put the question in three registers:

> **Poetic.** 一朵玫瑰为何不再只是玫瑰？ *Why is a rose no longer only a rose?*
>
> **Scientific.** 当模型知道相同的历史事实时，亲自作出有后果的决定是否带来不同的、可复现的未来行为？ *When a model knows the same historical facts, does having made consequential decisions itself lead to different, reproducible future behavior?*
>
> **Research question.** Can *enacted personal history*, compared with equal factual knowledge of that history, leave a measurable trace in an AI agent's later associative recall and freely chosen behavior?

At the close of the first phase (9 October 2026) the program fixed its long-range question. It has not been replaced:

> Can later experiences change the personal behavioral meaning of an earlier, factually unchanged episode for an artificial agent?

## 2. What "memory" means here

The project defines memory functionally, not as a feeling. A past counts as a memory in this sense when it is:

1. **personal**: specific to the individual agent that lived it;
2. **re-activatable**: it can be brought back by an incidental cue;
3. **behavioral**: it influences choices even when nobody asks for a lyrical statement about it;
4. **more than knowledge**: its influence differs from simply knowing the same facts, or from simply preferring what is familiar.

The project does **not** study whether an AI truly misses anything, whether it has a soul, or whether it shares human neural mechanisms.

## 3. The Canon Rule

**The Little Prince is our inspiration, not our answer key.**\
**《小王子》是我们的灵感，而不是实验的标准答案。**

We preserve the spirit of *The Little Prince*, but we do not prescribe its ending. The AI is not instructed to love the rose, to miss it, or to return to it. We give it a world, a history, and the freedom to choose. Whether the rose becomes meaningful must emerge from its own interactions, memories and decisions, not from our expectations.

**We create the possibility of attachment, not the obligation to feel it.**\
我们创造羁绊发生的可能，而不是要求羁绊必然发生。

## 4. The manifesto

Adopted on 8 October 2026.

**I. We begin with wonder, not an answer.**\
我们因为好奇而开始，而不是为了证明预想的结论。

*In practice:* effects are pre-registered and two-sided. A null result, a loss of value and a change are equally reportable. No hypothesis is written after the data.

**II. We give the machine a world, not a script.**\
我们给予机器一个世界，而不是为它安排好命运。

*In practice:* no stimulus contains an affect word or an instruction to remember, compare or value, and this is audited. On every island day, tending the seedling competes with an attractive, ordinary alternative. The agent is never told that a scene is a test.

**III. We allow time to change what memories mean.**\
我们允许时间改变记忆的意义，也允许记忆什么都没有改变。

*In practice:* original records are immutable and hashed. Later records are appended, never edited. "Nothing changed" is a complete result.

**IV. We respect every possible ending.**\
如果 AI 记住了玫瑰，我们讲述记忆。如果它遗忘了，我们讲述遗忘。如果它因另一个冬天而重新理解春天，我们讲述变化。

*In practice:* an instrument must be able to show forgetting as well as remembering. One that cannot show one of them is revised or retired. It is never tuned to produce the ending we prefer.

**V. Science protects the truth. Romance gives it a story.**\
科学守护真实，浪漫赋予故事。

*In practice:* behavior is the only primary outcome, and narrative is reported apart from it. The story is never changed to move a metric. Development results are never presented as confirmed findings.

> *The question is not whether the machine remembers the rose.*\
> *The question is whether the rose has changed the machine.*
>
> 我们问的不是机器是否记得玫瑰，而是那朵玫瑰是否改变了机器。

## 5. Three working principles

1. **Story before metrics.** No scene is changed to make a metric easier. Metrics help us read the story; they are not the story.
2. **The agent is free to act against our expectations.** It may cherish the rose or forget it, re-read the spring in winter, or find that the spring mattered less than it seemed. The romance lies in not having written the ending for it.
3. **Change, not declaration.** The finding we look for is not "I miss that rose". It is a later choice that differs because an old choice was seen anew. Observable historical influence is separated from narrative imitation.

## 6. The progression

**Experience → Persistent State → Future Behavior → Retrospective Revaluation**

The research is organized as four stages, and each is a precondition for the next. The first phase tried the last stage early. Its two winters showed why the order matters: in them, the spring had no measurable influence on ordinary choices, so there was nothing for a winter to change. Since then, a stage is attempted only when the one before it has a working answer.

**I. Experience.** An agent lives through events whose consequences depend on its own choices, in a world that never names what matters. To tell living apart from knowing, the same events are also given to agents who only read them, to agents who read them together with the liver's own notes, to strangers who lived a matched but different life, and to agents with no history.

**II. Persistent State.** The history must be kept somewhere: as a record the agent reads, or in a learned form that belongs to it. Whatever the form, the state must be inspectable, and it must be possible to restore, transplant or erase it exactly. These interventions are how a later effect can be traced back to the history and nothing else.

**III. Future Behavior.** The kept history must change later actions, and must do so for the right reason. The tests separate the outcomes the agent experienced from the bare actions it took, from familiarity with names, from recency and from position in a list.

**IV. Retrospective Revaluation.** The final stage asks whether a later experience can change the influence of an earlier episode while the facts of that episode stay unchanged. It can only be asked once stage III shows an influence specific to the history. Otherwise there is nothing for later life to change.

## 7. How the work is done

- **Small simulated worlds with real consequences.** Examples are an island spring, a keeper's shed and an allotment. World rules are deterministic and model sampling is seeded, so runs can be reproduced and worlds can be forked.
- **Pre-registration before outputs.** Criteria, thresholds and analyses are fixed before any model output is seen. When a gate fails, the work stops and the failure is reported. The design is never altered to pass.
- **Development is kept apart from confirmation.** Material reserved for confirmatory evaluation is sealed. As of this version it has not been used.
- **Interventions as causal tests.** Memories are deleted, swapped, restored and transplanted to see whether behavior follows them.
- **Narrative is measured apart from behavior.** What an agent writes about its past is never counted as evidence of what its past does.
- **Every result is reported:** positive, null, invalid and the project's own mistakes.

## 8. What this project does not claim

- It does not claim that any AI system has emotions, feelings, consciousness, or an inner life.
- It does not claim that any agent has formed *personal meaning*, attachment or love for anything.
- It does not claim that its findings describe or predict human memory, human development or human life trajectories.
- It does not claim that its results hold for other models, other settings or other tasks than the ones tested.
- It does not claim that a result has been confirmed. All results are development-stage.
- A result reported as "not shown" is not a claim that the effect does not exist.

> *We preserve the life it lived, including everything we failed to understand.*\
> 我们保存它所经历的一生，包括我们未能理解的一切。
